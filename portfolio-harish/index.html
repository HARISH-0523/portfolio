The Thermo-Autonomous Watch

A battery-free wearable powered by human body heat

  • → Explored a battery-free watch concept that uses body heat to generate electrical power, eliminating the need for conventional charging.
  • → Studied working principles of thermoelectric generators (TEGs), converting the temperature delta between skin and ambient air into usable electricity.
  • → Focused on self-sustaining operation and reduced dependency on batteries, targeting always-on wearable functionality.
  • → Analyzed suitability for outdoor activities and extreme environmental conditions, identifying optimal ΔT scenarios for maximum energy harvest.
